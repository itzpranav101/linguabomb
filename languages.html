Hindi

Sounds are very regular and the script is phonetic. Gendered nouns and verb agreement take time.

ScriptDevanagari (with Hinglish romanisation here)
DifficultyMedium
Culture tipUse "aap" (formal you) with elders and strangers. "Tum" and "tu" are for friends and younger people. A "namaste" with palms together is welcome almost anywhere.

Spanish

Pronounced as it is written, and lots of vocabulary overlaps with English. Verb conjugation is the main hurdle.

ScriptLatin alphabet (with ñ and accents)
DifficultyEasy-medium
Culture tipIn informal settings people often greet with a kiss on one or both cheeks. Whether to use "tú" or "usted" depends on the country, so listen first.

Mandarin

Grammar is simple, but four tones and thousands of characters make it a long road. Tones matter from day one.

ScriptSimplified Hanzi (with Pinyin here)
DifficultyChallenging
Culture tipOffer and receive gifts or business cards with both hands as a sign of respect. Be patient with tones: "mā" (mother) and "mǎ" (horse) differ only by tone.
